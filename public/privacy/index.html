Privacy at Pulse Check

Pulse Check handles audio and timing measurements in your browser.

Effective 6 September 2026.

Audio and measurements

Imported audio, detected onsets, taps, device labels, and calibration reports stay in your browser. Pulse Check does not upload them or store them on a server.

Demo storage and browser cache

The demo uses a separate browser key named demo:pulse-check:session. It is removed when you choose Start for real. The service worker caches application files so the tool can work offline after the first visit. Clearing site data removes the cache and any demo key.

Cookies and external services

Pulse Check uses no cookies, advertising, analytics, microphone access, or third-party runtime services. It does not need an account or API key.

Your exports

JSON exports are created on your device and saved only where you choose. They include the device label you enter and timing measurements, but no audio.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the public source repository linked in the footer.
