    Measurement limits
    
        
    Browser timing is an estimate
    
        
    Browsers cannot observe the instant sound leaves a speaker. Pulse Check measures your tap against the Web Audio clock, so output latency, display latency, touch sampling, and human response can mingle. Repeat on each target device, keep the cue mode and output route consistent, and use a physical loopback for release-critical measurement.
